# Lowtide Vigil

Once a year the sea pulls back from the drowned abbey of St. Maren for a single night. The Saint below, who has kept the dark asleep for a hundred years, is weeping. As the abbey's last bell-keeper you carry the vigil lantern down five flooded levels to relight her before dawn brings the water back. The town watches from the shore with candles. Each win lights another window on the shore screen and unlocks a starting lantern.
