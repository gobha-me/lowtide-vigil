# Lowtide Vigil

DRAFT - to be expanded

Once a year the sea pulls back from the drowned abbey of St. Maren for a
single night. The Saint below, who has kept the dark asleep for a hundred
years, is weeping. As the abbey's last bell-keeper, carry the vigil lantern
down five flooded levels to relight her before dawn brings the water back.
The town watches from the shore with candles.

1. **The Overgrown Nave** — Follow roots through the exposed nave; silence
   The Drowned Tolling to reopen the descent.
2. **The Ossuary Halls** — The bones of former keepers point toward the
   promise that bound the Saint below.
3. **The Belfry Crypt** — Recover the abbey's lost peal, and hear the Saint
   calling through its broken bells.
4. **The Drowned Galleries** — Wading past submerged memories, discover
   what the town has asked her to hold back.
5. **The Last Dark** — Relight the weeping Saint before the returning tide
   seals the abbey, and carry her answer to the shore.

The vigil lantern is the run's central promise: **flames = health = light
radius**. Its radius scales by `0.5 + 0.5 * hp / maxhp`; hurt the keeper and
the visible world contracts. Fire bolts and an invulnerable dash protect
that fragile light. Cleared-room signs offer KINDLE, EMBERS, ORDEAL, FONT,
and SHOP; a Kindle offers one of three ways to shape the lantern.

Each completed five-level vigil lights another window on the shore screen
and unlocks a starting lantern. The watching town should become a visible
record of hope, with a different lantern inviting another descent. This
meta progression is planned, not implemented in the Milestone 1 slice.
A Level 1 victory means the descent is open; it is not the Saint's rescue.

Art direction: warm lantern orange against drowned violet and sea green;
readable silhouettes, abbey arches, water marks and roots. Smooth toon art
is allowed; strict pixel art is optional. Priorities are **story > gameplay
~ controls > audio > graphics**. Put the tide, the vigil and the waiting
shore into the player's first moments and the ending before adding polish.
