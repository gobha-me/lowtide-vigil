Lowtide Vigil

Carry the last lantern down through five drowned floors. Every room changes the run.

How to play

Move
WASD · left stick · left thumb
Shoot
Hold mouse, or arrow keys to fire in that direction · right stick
Dash
Space, Shift or right click · you are untouchable mid-dash
Pause
Esc or P · M toggles sound
Doors
Cleared rooms open doors. The sign above each says what waits beyond: a kindle, embers, a ordeal, a font or the Candlewright's shop.
Flames
Your flames are your life, and your lantern dims as they go out.
